Tiki-Tak Passing — W2 Progression: Give-and-Go 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual — Block 3

Groups of 3. Same one-touch pace from W1. W2 pattern: A passes to B, B one-touches back to A, A immediately plays into C — give-and-go triangle. But the receiver (B) must check away first for 2 steps before checking back to receive. No standing still waiting for the ball.

  • Receiver checks away 2 steps, plants, checks back sharp — then the pass comes
  • Passer waits for the check — timing is the skill. Don't play until the check happens.
  • If the group gets 10+ consecutive touches: they call the count out loud together
5 – 15
Warm-Up: Dynamic Movement Circuit 10 min

Players move through a 20m corridor in waves, one group every 10 seconds. Run through each movement, jog back, repeat. Previews the Station A agility shapes so no time is lost on explanation later.

  • High knees: 20m at medium pace — drive the knees up, arms pumping
  • Bounding: long powerful strides, push off the ball of the foot, land balanced
  • Lateral shuffle: side-to-side across two cones 4m apart — stay low, don't cross the feet
  • Quick feet through cones: 8 cones spaced 30cm apart — fast small steps, every foot in every space
  • Acceleration sprint: 10m at full pace — explode from a standing start
💡
Coaching tip: This warm-up is also setup time for Station A. Have the agility shapes in place before you start so the group walking past them can see the layout. No explanation needed — they'll recognize it when they rotate over.

Technical Stations: Agility/Speed · Pressing Patience · Aerial Control 45 min · 15 min per station

Station A is the Agility/Speed circuit — four drills rotated through in 15 minutes. Station B deepens Pressing Patience with a 3-man shape. Station C continues aerial control in motion.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Agility & Speed Circuit

Four drills in rotation, ~3 min each. Players cycle through all four. Full intensity — this is the point. Rest between reps, not between drills. See drill descriptions below. Cue: "Explode. Stick it. Recover."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Pressing Patience: 3-Man Shape

Scale up from Tuesday's 2-man press to a 3-man shape in a larger zone (~15×15m). Striker closes the ball, CM covers the inside pass lane, wide player holds width to cut the outside option. Three attackers try to play through the press — 3v3. No diving. Force them sideways. Cue: "Funnel them sideways. Patient."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Aerial: Half-Volley in Motion

Player walks in slowly; ball is served slightly ahead of them so they must move into it to trap. Chest trap while walking — the body keeps moving through the contact. Progress: player jogs in. The ball doesn't wait for you — you move to meet it. No heading. Cue: "Don't stop to trap. Move into it."

Station A — Agility Drill Rotation (Pete)
Drill 1 · Short Hurdle Step-Overs → Sprint

5 low hurdles (or cones) spaced 50cm apart — two-foot step-overs, stay on toes. Immediately after last hurdle: full sprint to a cone 10m away. No pause between hurdles and sprint.

Drill 2 · Skipping High-Knee Bounds

Skipping with exaggerated high knee drive — each skip is a power bound, not a soft skip. Arms drive opposite to the knee. 20m down, jog back. Focus on height and hang time per bound.

Drill 3 · Zig-Zag Bounds + Balance Pause

5 cones in a zig-zag, ~2m apart. Player bounds between cones landing on one foot, pauses and holds balance for 1 full second before the next bound. Stick the landing — wobble means another rep.

Drill 4 · Kneeling-Start Sprint

Player kneels on both knees, hands at sides. On coach's call: drive to feet and sprint 15m at full pace. First 3 steps are the point — explosive transition from still to full speed. Time from voice to cone if possible.

SSG: 3v3 + GK + Target Player — Two Simultaneous Games 20 min

Second appearance of this SSG. Players know the format now — no re-introduction needed. Coaching focus: does the target player actually "show" (move to receive)? Does Team 2 counter-attack immediately on interception?

Coaching Focus — Show & Counter
  • Target player must move: if the target is standing still waiting for the ball — stop play and name it. "Show for the ball — don't stand there."
  • Instant counter: the moment Team 2 intercepts, the whole team should already be moving forward. Stop play if there's a 2-second hesitation after a turnover.
  • Reward the first team that executes a clean counter-attack with verbal recognition from the sideline
  • Rotate target player and GK roles every 4–5 minutes
⚡
Cue: "Show for the ball. Instant counter." Use these two phrases repeatedly from the sideline. The boys should start predicting what you're going to say — that means the habit is forming.

Closing Game

Standard knockout. All 19, one ball each. After Agility Day — legs are tired. That's fine. Sprint to the ball, sprint back. No walking. Celebrate the last two players standing.
