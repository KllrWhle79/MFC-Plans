Tiki-Tak — Speed & Intensity Opening 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual

Groups of 2 or 3, 6 feet apart. One-touch, maximum pace. Triangle flow for groups of 3. Penalty for holding: 5 toe-taps and restart. Week 3: coaches should be able to run a full rep themselves — lead by doing.

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Transition Chase Rondo — Finish Required 10 min

Same transition rondo. Week 3: when defenders win the ball, the 2v1 must end with a combination before the shot — a wall pass or give-and-go. Directly links to Station B today.

  • 5v2 in 10×10m. Swap on intercept or 8 passes.
  • Defenders win ball → 2v1 → must complete a wall pass or give-and-go before the finish
  • Progression (min 10): 3 defenders inside
💡
Coaching tip: The combination requirement will cause some defenders to slow down after winning the ball — that's fine. The instinct to look for the partner immediately after winning is exactly what you want to build. Name it when you see it.

Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Pass & Move (2 Defenders)

Triangle passing with 1 defender — same as Tuesday. Week 3 top group: 2 defenders. Passing player must identify the free player before receiving. New: 1 defender, unlimited touches, scanning call every receive. Exp: 2 defenders, 2-touch, must name the free player out loud before receiving.

Blue · Coach Brent · Station B — Wall Pass (Beat the Defender)

Same give-and-go. Week 3: defender tracks A's run and actively tries to intercept the return pass. Attacker must time the run to arrive before the defender. New: defender jogs tracking — focus on timing the run into space. Exp: defender fully active, A must beat defender to the return — one-touch finish if possible.

White · Coach David · Station C — Press-Back 3v3 (5-Sec Window)

Press window tightens from 6 to 5 seconds for all players. Coach counts aloud for new players, silently times for experienced. Combination required before shooting after winning the ball. New: 5-second window, coach counts — press before the count finishes. Exp: 5-second window, win ball → combination → shoot, coach times silently.

Over the River — Two Games · 7-Pass Build 20 min

Same two-game format. Week 3: build count raises to 7 passes. 2-touch limit now in effect for all players from the start (introduced at 10 min in Week 2 — now it's the baseline).

  • 7 passes required before switching through the middle
  • 2-touch limit in the outer grid from the first rep — no exceptions
  • After 5 seconds: 1 defender from Team C enters
  • Successful switch → full reset → cycle repeats
  • Progression (min 10): 2 defenders enter after 5 seconds (up from 1)
💡
Coaching tip: With 2-touch now the baseline, teams will find 7 passes harder. That's correct. The pressure of 2-touch forces quicker thinking and better positioning. At 10 minutes add the second defender: "Two defenders after five seconds — let's go." No other explanation needed.
