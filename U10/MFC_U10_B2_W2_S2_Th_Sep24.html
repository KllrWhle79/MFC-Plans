0 – 5
Tiki-Tak — Speed & Intensity Opening 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual

Groups of 2 or 3, 6 feet apart. One-touch passes, maximum pace. On toes, body open. Groups of 3: triangle flow A→B→C→B→A. Penalty for holding: 5 toe-taps and restart. Week 2: raise the energy standard — if a group's pace drops, call "Tiki-Tak pace!" once and move on.

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Transition Chase Rondo 10 min

Same Transition Chase Rondo as Week 1. Week 2 progression: when defenders win the ball, they attack 2v1 — and the 2v1 must end with a shot or a cross. No holding possession after winning it.

  • 5v2 rondo in 10×10m. Swap on intercept or 8 passes.
  • When defenders intercept: 2v1 immediately — must finish with a shot or cross attempt
  • Progression (min 10): 3 defenders inside — forces quicker decisions from outside players
💡
Coaching tip: The finish condition makes the 2v1 purposeful — players can't just dribble around. Watch for the moment a defender wins the ball and immediately looks up to find their partner. That scan is the whole lesson from Tuesday transferring in real time.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Pass & Move · Wall Pass · Press-Back 3v3 45 min · 15 min per station

Thursday stations shift to passing combinations. All three focus on moving the ball quickly under active pressure. Week 2: defender tracks the runner in Station B. Station C press window introduced.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays. Groups rotate: Gold → Blue → White → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Pass & Move Under Pressure

Triangle passing — player passes to the next cone and follows their pass to that position. 1 active defender tries to intercept. Scanning call mandatory before every receive. New: unlimited touches, defender jogs — focus on the scanning call and following the pass. Exp: 2-touch, defender fully active, must identify the free player before receiving.

Blue · Coach Brent · Station B — Wall Pass (Give & Go)

A passes to wall player B, then sprints into space to receive the return. Week 2: 1 defender now tracks A's run. Attacker must beat the defender to the space. New: no defender — wall holds 1 second, A sprints freely, focuses on timing the run. Exp: one-touch wall pass, defender tracking from rep 1, A must time the run to beat the defender.

White · Coach David · Station C — Press-Back 3v3

3v3 in a zone with mini-goals. Team losing the ball must win it back within 6 seconds. Coach counts aloud. Bonus point for winning ball and scoring directly. New: 6-second press window, coach counts — focus on immediate pressure after turnover. Exp: same 6-second window but must play a combination (wall pass or give-and-go) before shooting after winning the ball back.

New Players — pressure entering
  • Station A: unlimited touches, call "checking!" before every receive
  • Station B: no defender — time the run and focus on the sprint into space
  • Station C: 6-second window, coach counts — press immediately, don't wait
  • Coaches: name the scan when you see it, every time
Experienced — combinations
  • Station A: 2-touch, identify free player before receiving — call their name
  • Station B: one-touch wall, beat defender to the return pass
  • Station C: win ball → combination → shoot — no direct shots after winning
  • Speed of play is the standard: if it feels slow, it is slow
💡
Station coaching style: Demo once, then play. Thursday energy cue: "Tiki-Tak pace!" Use it once per station if the group loses intensity — they know what it means.
60 – 80
Over the River — Two Games · 6-Pass Build 20 min

Same two-game Over the River format. Week 2 progression: raise the build count from 5 to 6 passes before switching. Everything else identical — 5 seconds then 1 defender enters, full team resets on successful switch.

  • 6 passes required in the outer grid before switching through the middle (up from 5)
  • After 5 seconds: 1 defender from Team C enters the grid with the ball
  • Successful switch → all of Team C resets to the middle → cycle repeats
  • Progression (min 10): 2-touch limit inside the outer grid for all players
💡
Coaching tip: The extra pass forces the team to stay composed under pressure for slightly longer. Watch for teams who rush to switch before 6 — call the count out loud for the first few reps. At 10 minutes, add the 2-touch limit without stopping play: "Two touches only — go!" and count with them until they self-regulate.

Closing Ritual

All 19. GK sets angle. Panenka and chip attempts welcome — crowd decides if it counts for style. When 4 remain: "The Final Four!" Sprint to ball, sprint back into line — hustle rule enforced from rep 1.
