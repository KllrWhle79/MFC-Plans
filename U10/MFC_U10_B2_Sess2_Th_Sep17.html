0 – 5
Tiki-Tak Passing — Speed & Intensity Opening 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual — Block 2 onward

Groups of 2 or 3, standing 6 feet (2m) apart. One-touch passes back and forth, as fast as possible. High intensity from rep one. Players on their toes, weight forward, ready for the ball before it arrives.

  • Stance: on the balls of the feet, slightly bent knees, body open to receive
  • Contact: inside of the foot, firm surface, keep the ball on the ground
  • Speed: the ball should barely stop — one touch and it goes back. No holding.
  • Groups of 3: A passes to B, B one-touches to C, C one-touches back to B, B to A — triangle flow
  • If the ball stops or a player takes more than one touch: 5 toe-taps and restart
💡
Coaching tip: Demo once with one player for 30 seconds, then send everyone. The pace is the point. When a group gets a long clean sequence going, name the energy: "That's the tempo we want in every drill today."
5 – 15
Warm-Up: Transition Chase Rondo 10 min

5v2 rondo in a 10×10m square. When the middle pair wins the ball, they immediately become attackers and attack 2v1 against the nearest outside player. Press-and-go concept taught through play.

  • 5 outside players, 2 defenders inside. Swap on intercept or 8 passes.
  • When defenders intercept: they immediately drive out and attack 2v1 against one outside player who chases
  • Outcome of the 2v1 doesn't matter — the concept is instant transition from pressing to attacking
  • Progression (min 10): 3 defenders inside — harder to keep the ball, faster transitions
💡
Coaching tip: Don't stop to explain the transition concept. Let them discover it. After the third or fourth transition, pause briefly: "What just happened? They won the ball and immediately attacked — that's the press-and-go." One sentence, then restart.

Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — 1v1 Defending (Pressing Angle)

Same 1v1 channel as Tuesday. Thursday focus: after the defender wins or forces the ball out, they immediately drive to a target cone — win the ball AND go forward. Builds press-and-recover habit. New: shadow-defend, focus on positioning; after each rep point to where you'd go if you won it. Exp: contest, win ball, immediately dribble to target cone at pace.

Blue · Coach Brent · Station B — Escape Pressure (Corner Setup)

Same corner setup as Tuesday — attacker backed into the corner, defender passes well then follows to pressure, two gates at 45°. Thursday: defender reads the first touch and tries to cut off the chosen gate — no diving, but genuine pressure. New: same as Tuesday — good pass, first touch, choose a gate, unlimited touches. Exp: 2-touch limit after first touch; defender actively cuts off one gate without diving — attacker must commit and accelerate through the other.

White · Coach David · Station C — 4v2 Rondo

Same 4v2 as Tuesday. Thursday: beat the group's Tuesday record. Experienced players must call the name of the receiver before passing. New: unlimited touches, try to beat Tuesday's count. Exp: 2-touch mandatory, call name of receiver before passing — no call = redo the pass.

Station B — Escape Pressure: Quick Reference (Coach Brent)
  • Setup: Attacker backed into a corner — touchline and end line behind them. Two gates at ~45° from each other in front (one up the touchline, one up the field). Defender ~10 feet away.
  • The pass: Defender plays a clean, well-weighted pass to the attacker's feet. If the pass is poor, redo it. Cue: "Give them something they can work with."
  • Defender follows and pressures — does not crash in: close the space steadily, force the attacker toward one gate, no diving or lunging.
  • Thursday variation: defender reads the attacker's first touch and tries to cut off the chosen gate. Attacker must commit and drive through.
  • One cue, every rep: "Where is the pressure? Touch away from it."

60 – 80
SSG: 5v5 + 5v4 — Pressing & Pass Condition 20 min

19 players split into 4 teams — roughly 5, 5, 5, and 4. Two simultaneous games: Game 1 is 5v5, Game 2 is 5v4. All four teams are on the field the whole time. No subs. Thursday conditions: 2 passes before shooting + press on every turnover.

  • Condition 1 (all): complete 2 passes before shooting — coaches count aloud
  • Condition 2 (all): press on every turnover — coaches call "PRESS!" on every loss
  • Rotate opponents after 10 minutes — teams swap pitches so everyone faces a different opponent
  • Final 4 min: drop both conditions — free play, no coaching. Let it breathe.
💡
Coaching tip: Watch for the 2-pass condition creating a better shot angle — teams who keep the ball with two clean passes will have a stronger chance. If you see a well-executed press that leads directly to a goal, freeze briefly: "That press created that goal. From Station A today." Connect it explicitly once, then let it go.
80 – 90
⚽ Knockout — The Final Four 10 min
Closing Ritual — same every session through Block 4

All 19. Standard format. GK sets angle before every shot. When the field narrows to 4 players — announce "The Final Four!" — run semi-final shots then a final. Eliminated players lead the crowd chant for the final. Hustle rule: every player sprints into position at the ball and sprints back into the crowd immediately after their shot. No walking between turns. Coaches set the tone from the first shot — if the pace drops, call it once: "Hustle!" and players self-correct.
