5 – 15
Warm-Up: Sharks & Minnows — Hunt in Threes 10 min

Week 3 Sharks & Minnows: sharks now press in threes. One pressures, one cuts the escape, one holds depth. Introduces the concept of defensive shape without a lecture.

  • 5 sharks pressing in groups of 3 (with 1 pair), 14 minnows. Sharks rotate groupings every 2 minutes.
  • Minnow loses ball → becomes a shark. Last 3 minnows win.
  • Progression (min 10): minnows may only dribble — no passing — forces individual escape under pressure
💡
Coaching tip: Don't explain the three-man press shape — let them discover it. After the first round, ask the sharks: "What worked? What didn't?" Let two players answer. Then restart. The concept surfaces itself.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: 1v1 Defending · Escape Pressure · 4v2 / 5v2 Rondo 45 min · 15 min per station

Week 3 scaling: full pressure everywhere. Station A defender wins and plays to a named target. Station B attacker must commit and accelerate — no hesitation. Station C top group upgrades to 5v2.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays. Groups rotate: Gold → Blue → White → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — 1v1 Defending (Win & Play Out)

Narrowed channel from Week 2. Defender wins the ball AND immediately plays it to a named target player at the side of the channel — no dribbling to cone, an actual pass. Builds transition instinct. New: win ball or force out-of-bounds — both count. Point at the target after winning. Exp: win ball, play to named target immediately — one touch to target if possible.

Blue · Coach Brent · Station B — Escape Pressure (Commit & Accelerate)

Same corner setup. Week 3: after choosing a gate, attacker must accelerate hard through it — not just dribble through. Defender can now shift to cover one gate after the first touch. Attacker must read which gate is open and commit fully. New: same as Week 2 — first touch chooses gate, drive through. Exp: 2-touch limit, defender shifts after first touch — attacker reads the shift and drives hard through the open gate. No slowing down at the gate.

White · Coach David · Station C — 4v2 Rondo / 5v2 Top Group

Standard 4v2 continues. Top group of experienced players upgrades to 5v2 — larger square, more angles. "Checking!" mandatory for all, defenders fully active. New: 4v2, defenders active — focus on keeping the sequence going under real pressure. Exp: 5v2 in 10×10m square, 2-touch mandatory, "checking!" mandatory — if either is skipped, possession switches immediately.

New Players — full pressure
  • Station A: win or force out-of-bounds — both are successful defending
  • Station B: first touch decides, then drive — don't slow at the gate
  • Station C: 4v2, keep counting your passes aloud, "checking!" every time
  • Coaches: max one freeze per 15 min — only for a genuinely great moment
Experienced — speed and decision
  • Station A: win ball, one-touch pass to target — no pausing
  • Station B: read the defender's shift, accelerate hard through the open gate
  • Station C: 5v2, 2-touch, "checking!" — skipped call = immediate possession switch
  • If it feels comfortable, the constraints need to tighten

60 – 80
SSG: 5v5 + 5v4 — Press & Shape 20 min

19 players, 4 teams (5,5,5,4). Two simultaneous games. Week 3: positional starts continue. Additional condition: after winning the ball, team must play through their CM before shooting.

  • Teams start in 2-3-1 shape as set by coach
  • Press condition on every turnover — coaches call "PRESS!"
  • New condition: after winning the ball, the next pass must go to or through the CM before a shot is taken. Encourages building through the middle.
  • Rotate opponents at 10 minutes. Final 4 min: free play.
💡
Coaching tip: The CM condition will feel forced at first. Don't enforce it rigidly — if the CM is genuinely out of position, let the play continue. The goal is awareness, not rule-following. When a team naturally plays through the middle and scores, that's the moment to name.
