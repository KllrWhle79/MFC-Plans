Tuesday Opening Ritual — Block 3

Players juggle freely on arrival. Feet only. No target, no counting aloud unless a player wants to call their own best. W4 twist: if anyone reaches 20 consecutive touches, they set down the ball and cheer on the others until the 5 minutes is up.

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Pressure Rondo 10 min

Returning from W2 Tuesday. 4v2 in a 10×10m square. Defenders stay side-on, no diving. Primes the defending stations and the pressing decisions that will appear in the SSG.

  • 4 attackers keep possession vs. 2 side-on defenders in a 10×10m square
  • Defenders: stay side-on at all times. No diving across. No ball-watching from behind.
  • When a defender wins the ball, they immediately swap with the attacker who lost it — no pause
  • Progress at 7 minutes: shrink to 8×8m. Same rules. Tighter = more decisions per second.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Agility Final · Sprint Free-Choice · Defending Consecutive 45 min · 15 min per station

Station A is the second and final agility circuit — adds reaction start and direction call. Station B is self-directed: players choose their own reps. Station C runs three consecutive 1v1 defending rounds without stopping between.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Agility/Speed Circuit: Reaction Start + Direction Call

Same four drills from W2 (hurdle step-overs, skipping bounds, zig-zag+pause, kneeling start). New layer: on the kneeling-start sprint, coach calls "LEFT" or "RIGHT" at the 8m mark — player cuts in that direction to a cone and continues. Also add: two players race the kneeling sprint side-by-side once (pure competition). Cue: "React first. Think second."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Full-Sprint Dribbling: Self-Directed

All three gate options available (straight gate, front-defender variant, reactive gate). Player chooses which one to run each rep. Coach stands back. Minimum 8 reps in 15 minutes — that's the only rule. The player decides their challenge level. Brent observes and gives one short note per player when the group is done. Cue: "You decide. You push."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Defending Body Shape: 3 Consecutive Reps

1v1 defending. Instead of swapping roles after each rep, the defender defends three rounds in a row against three different attackers, back-to-back with no reset. Challenges the mantra under fatigue. After three consecutive defenses, the first defender rotates out. Mantra aloud on every rep: "Side — Low — Fast — Slow." Cue: "Still correct on rep 3. Say the mantra every time."

Station A W4 Upgrades — Reaction + Race (Pete)
Kneeling Start + Direction Cut

Same kneeling start — but at the 8m cone, coach calls "LEFT" or "RIGHT". Player must cut to that side and run to a wide cone 5m off-centre. Can't predict the call; must read and react at speed.

Side-by-Side Kneeling Race

Two players kneel next to each other. Coach calls "GO!" — first to the 15m cone wins. Once per player per rotation. Pure competition adds intensity the drill doesn't have on its own.

Zig-Zag + One Extra Bound

Returning from W2 — but after the final zig-zag cone, player immediately bounds one more time (unpredictable direction — coach points left or right as they land the last cone). Tests sustained reaction after a fatiguing sequence.

Hurdle Step-Overs → Sprint → 180° Turn

Same hurdle sequence as W2, but after the 10m sprint: player touches the cone and immediately turns 180° and sprints back 5m. The full sequence is hurdles → sprint → touch → return sprint. No stopping at the far cone.

SSG: 3v3 + GK + Target Player — No Coaching Cues 20 min · Two Simultaneous Games

Final scheduled 3v3+Target appearance. Full rules in place. No cues from the sideline — coaches observe and record. The players know the format; let them demonstrate what they've learned.

Full Rules — Reminder
  • GK receives ball, rolls to Team 1. Team 1 builds toward the target player at far end. Target player must "show" — move to receive.
  • If Team 2 intercepts: immediate counter-attack — no reset, play on.
  • Goal scored only by receiving the ball from the target player and then scoring (target player may not score directly).
  • Rotate GK and target player every 4 minutes. Two games running simultaneously — Field 1 and Field 2.
No-Coaching Protocol
  • Coaches stay silent on both fields for the full 20 minutes. No encouragement, no cues, no corrections — observe only.
  • Each coach watches one field and takes a mental note of 2 specific players: one making strong decisions, one who needs a conversation.
  • After Knockout, coaches share notes with each other (2 min). Individual conversations happen at the next session or practice.
