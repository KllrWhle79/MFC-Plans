Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Fast Dribbling W5 · Open Gates

Final fast dribbling station: 3 cone gates set up at angles. Coach calls out a gate number after the player starts the dribble. Player must cut to reach that gate with speed. No set pattern — full reaction dribbling. Celebrate: "Five weeks of dribbling. Look how much sharper you are than Week 1."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Back Foot W5 · Player's Choice

W5 capstone: same 2v1 live scenario from W4, but receiver chooses any turn they prefer — Cruyff, hook, step-over, simple body feint, whatever works. No prescribed technique. Coach watches and names what players do: "That was a Cruyff — did you know that?" Purpose is building ownership of the skill. Cue: "Make a decision. Trust your body."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Set Piece Combined Review

Combined station: 5 throw-in scenarios (goal-side positioning) + 5 corner scenarios (GK calls near/far, defenders race to position). Two groups: attackers on throw or corner, defenders positioning. Rotate roles. Coach asks players to narrate: "What did you check before the ball was thrown?" Cue: "Scan. Position. Hold. Don't jump early."

Gold — W5 Press (Full Autonomy)

GK calls press shape, executes, forwards react. Coaches say nothing. After each sequence that works: "That's it." After breakdowns: "What happened?" and let the GK explain. Goal: 5 consecutive clean sequences with zero coach prompting.

Blue — W5 Sequence (Full Loop)

GK → CB back-foot → outside back → winger decides → CM anticipates. After a full sequence that reaches the CM in a good position, freeze and show the shape: "This is what build-out looks like. This is Block 4." Let the players own the moment.
