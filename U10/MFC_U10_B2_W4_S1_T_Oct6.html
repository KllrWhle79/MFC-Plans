SSG: 5v5 + 5v4 — Full Pressure · Press & Shape 25 min

19 players, 4 teams. Two simultaneous games. Full pressure all Block 2 conditions active simultaneously: positional starts, press on turnover, CM condition. Longest SSG of the block — give them the extra time to express what they've learned.

  • Positional 2-3-1 starts. Press on every turnover. Play through CM before shooting.
  • Rotate opponents at 12 minutes (slightly longer round than previous weeks).
  • Final 6 min: drop all conditions — free play. No coaching. Let Block 2 express itself.
💡
Coaching tip: During the free play final 6 minutes, coaches step back completely. No calls, no "PRESS!", no corrections. Just watch. What you see in those 6 minutes — unprompted, unconditioned — is exactly what Block 2 built. Remember specific moments for Thursday's send-off.
