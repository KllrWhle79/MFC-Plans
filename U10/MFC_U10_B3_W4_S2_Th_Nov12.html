0 – 5
Tiki-Tak Passing — W4 Final: Full Triangle, Shadow Defender, All Move After Pass 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual — Block 3 W4 · Full Progression

Groups of 4 — 3 passers in a triangle plus 1 shadow defender who follows but never intercepts. Every player must move to a new position immediately after passing — no staying in place. The shadow defender mirrors the ball, staying 1m away. Purpose: passing and movement under permanent positional pressure. No pausing, no re-setting. If the group keeps moving continuously for 60 seconds, they win the round.

  • Shadow defender follows the ball — not the player. Ball moves, shadow moves.
  • Every passer: one step in any direction immediately after the ball leaves your foot.
  • This is the most complex Tiki-Tak variation of Block 3. If a group struggles, drop back to W3 (triangles without the shadow) for 1 minute, then re-introduce.
5 – 15
Warm-Up: Combination — Free Dribble Into Pressure Rondo 10 min

Block 3 has two warm-up formats: free dribble (Tuesdays) and transition/rondo (Thursdays). This finale blends both. 5 minutes of free dribble with all three moves available; then transition immediately into a 4v2 rondo. No break between — move as a team.

  • Minutes 5–10: free dribble in a large grid. All moves available (scissor, sole stop, front-foot cut). No instruction — coaches observe.
  • At the 10-minute whistle: all players immediately move into their rondo squares (4v2, 10×10m). No explanation needed — they know this format.
  • Minutes 10–15: rondo at full intensity. If a defender wins the ball, the attacker who lost it becomes the next defender immediately.
💡
Coaching tip: The dribble-to-rondo transition is itself a transition exercise. Watch who organizes quickly and who needs direction. Those are the leaders for the SSG in 45 minutes. No coaching during either segment — just observe.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Pressing Consolidation · Sprint + GK Chip · Aerial Full Sequence 45 min · 15 min per station

Each station is a final, full-speed version of its Block 3 progression. No new concepts — consolidation and performance. Coaches give one piece of feedback per player at the end of their 15 minutes, then the group rotates.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Pressing: Game-Speed 3v3 Consolidation

Full 3v3 press. No cue from Pete about which shape to use — pressing team calls the shape themselves before each rep ("Close only!" / "Channel!" / "Full press!"). Attackers try to play through. 3 minutes on, 1 minute off, 3 sets. Pete gives one note per player, on their best press or their most frequent error, at the end. Cue: "You choose the shape. You call it. Now press."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Sprint Dribbling: GK Chip Into Space

New scenario for the finale: GK (Brent or a player) chips the ball into open space 20m away. Player sprints, arrives first, takes the first touch long in the direction of a goal (or end cone), then shoots or dribbles past a final gate. The whole sequence — react, sprint, long touch, finish — must be completed in one continuous movement. No stopping to re-position. Cue: "Sprint onto it. Big touch. Don't stop."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Aerial Final: Chest → Lay-off → Teammate Shoots

Three-player sequence at game speed. David chips to Player A from 10m. A chest traps, immediately lays off to Player B (who has moved to receive — no waiting). B shoots on goal or a target cone. Rotate: A→B→server. All serves from the foot — no hand tosses. No heading at any point. Cue: "Trap — lay off — shoot. No pauses."

60 – 80
SSG: Coach's Choice — Dog Fight or 3v3 + GK + Target 20 min

Run whichever SSG format the coaching staff wants to observe on the block's last day. Both are fully established. Decide before the session based on what Thursday's observation from W4 S1 showed — if transition decisions need more reps, run Dog Fight. If build-and-connect needed work, run 3v3+Target.

Option A: Dog Fight (if chosen)
  • Wide & short. 3 pug goals each end. Whole team. 2 balls. All restarts apply.
  • Cue: "Win it — pick a goal — go!" — the sequence from W3. Let the players call it to each other this time.
Option B: 3v3 + GK + Target (if chosen)
  • Two simultaneous games. GK rolls to Team 1. Target player shows. Immediate counter on intercept.
  • Let it run with no cues. Final observation opportunity for individual decision-making under pressure.
⚡
Either way: the last 5 minutes of the SSG (75–80), pull back all coaching. Pure competition. Players own it. That's what Block 3 has been building toward.
80 – 90
Knockout + Block 3 Close 10 min
Final Game of Block 3

Standard knockout — full energy, full competition. Keep it to 7 minutes so there's time for a block close. Coaches are allowed to celebrate and cheer during knockout.

Block 3 Close — 2–3 Minutes Maximum
Gather the group after knockout. Short and specific — no speeches. Cover three things:

1. What we worked on: "This block was about transitions — reading when the ball changes hands and reacting before anyone tells you to."

2. Name something you saw: One specific moment from today's SSG. A real name, a real play. "I saw [player] start running before the tackle even happened. That's the point."

3. What's next: Block 4 starts in two weeks. New block, new theme. Don't preview the content — just leave the door open. "Two weeks, we start something new. Come back ready."
