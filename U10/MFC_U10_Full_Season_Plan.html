Two-Part Season

This plan covers the first half of the season (Aug–Dec). Season 2 begins the second week of January and runs through end of April, building on the foundations established here. Block 1 includes whiteboard/club soccer education sessions — from Block 2 onward the approach is fully European: play-based, coach-as-observer, no lines and no lectures.

Team Structure
Gold
1st Team · Coach A
3 experienced + 3 newer players. Mixed intentionally — experienced players model ball habits and positional instincts for newer teammates.
Blue
2nd Team · Coach B
3 experienced + 3 newer players. Emphasis on building comfort in structured drills and encouraging newer players to compete confidently.
White
3rd Team · Coach C
3 experienced + 3 newer players. Experienced players serve as peer models during partner and small-sided work. Rotate group membership every block.

☀️

Phoenix Heat Protocol — August & September

Temperatures regularly exceed 105°F. Keep intensity low in Weeks 1–3. Mandatory water break every 15–18 minutes minimum. The whiteboard segment is intentionally the cool-down window — players sit in shade with water in hand. Consider scheduling sessions after 6pm if possible. Ice towels on the sideline recommended. Watch for red faces and heavy breathing. Never skip a break.

Phoenix Heat Protocol
Aug–Sep Wks 1–2: low intensity, one session/week, 70 min, mandatory water every 15–18 min. Whiteboard time in shade is a structured cool-down window. Never skip a break.
🎯
Juggling Opens Every Session
5 minutes, every session, no exceptions. Self-directed, personal bests, coaches count alongside players. Latecomers join without disruption. The gathering ritual that gets 18 kids focused before anything else begins.
🔄
3-Station Rotation (Blocks 2–4)
Each coach owns one concept per session. Teams of 6 rotate through all three stations (~15 min each). No waiting, no lines. Same stations all block, scaled weekly. Each coach becomes expert in their concept across the block.
🖊️
Whiteboard Block 1 Only
Club soccer education — rules, positions, set pieces — is concentrated in the first block. From Block 2: European play-based approach. All teaching happens through the game itself. Coaches guide through questions, not lectures.
⚽
Knockout Closes Every Session
Penalty-style shootout, all 18 players, every session. GK rotates every round. Eliminated players become the crowd. No coaching during Knockout. This ritual builds competition culture, GK confidence, and consistent session closure across the entire season.
⚖️
Specific Recognition
"I loved how you checked your shoulder before that pass" is 10× more powerful than "great job." Name the player, name the action, name why it mattered. Never compare players to each other — compare each to their own previous attempt.

Block 1 · Technical block drills · same drill all 4 weeks, scaling increases
Gold · Coach A · Weeks 1–4

Ball mastery circuit

Sole rolls · inside-outside · L-turns · named skill moves

Each player has their own ball and runs the circuit simultaneously. At each cone: a touch sequence (sole rolls, inside-outside, L-turn). Coach calls out a specific move to do at the next cone.

New players

Walk pace, any foot, coach runs alongside first rep

Experienced

Jog pace, weak foot only Wk3+, time challenge

Blue · Coach B · Weeks 1–4

Paired first touch

Partner rolls · receiver controls · returns

3 pairs work simultaneously at 6m. Roller feeds ball slowly to feet. Receiver stops with inside foot and returns. Swap after every 5 reps. Coach circulates between pairs.

New players

Just stop ball cleanly. Slow delivery to feet only

Experienced

Receive and turn in one motion. Ball in air Wk3+

White · Coach C · Weeks 1–4

Dribble slalom

Weave through 6 cones · turn · return

One player goes at a time. Weave through all 6 cones (40cm spacing), turn at the end, and return through the same cones. Others wait and encourage. Personal time challenge.

New players

Walk pace, any foot, coach alongside first rep

Experienced

Run pace, weak foot only, beat personal time

Gold · Coach A · Weeks 2–4 (shielding)

Shield · scan · exit pass

Protect ball · call "checking!" · name target · play out

3-player drill. Ball carrier shields from 1 defender for 8 seconds while scanning for the free target. Must call "checking!" and the target's name before playing the exit pass.

New players

Defender walks only. Target calls for ball verbally

Experienced

Active defender. Must name target before passing. 2 defenders Wk3+

White · Coach C · Weeks 3–4

1v1 to gate

Beat defender with skill move · score through gate

Attacker dribbles at defender and must use a skill move to beat them before scoring through the gate. 3 pairs running simultaneously. Swap attacker/defender every 2 reps.

New players

Defender passive (shadows). Larger gate. Any skill move

Experienced

Active defender. Normal gate. Named specific skill move required

Blue · Coach B · Weeks 3–4

Triangle passing

Pass → immediately run to next cone → receive

3 cones ~10m apart in a triangle. Pass to the next cone and immediately sprint to that cone. Ball always moves faster than the players. Keep tempo up and rhythm continuous.

New players

Slow tempo. Remind to run after passing. No time pressure

Experienced

2-touch limit. Call teammate's name before passing Wk4

Block 2 · 3-station rotation · same stations all 4 weeks, scaling increases
Station A · Coach A · Day 1

1v1 channel defending

Goal-side · show outside · no dive-in

Attacker dribbles in 3m channel toward gate. Defender stays goal-side, shows attacker to outside. Swap roles every 2 reps. Scales to active tackle and narrower channel.

New players

Shadow only, no contact. Channel 5m wide Wk1

Experienced

Active tackle allowed. 2m channel. Win ball + dribble to cone Wk3

Station B · Coach B · Day 1

4v2 rondo with scanning

"Checking!" mandatory before every receive

4 outside keep ball from 2 middle defenders in 8m square. Scanning call mandatory before every receive — no call means the pass is replayed. Group tracks consecutive pass record.

New players

Unlimited touches. Scanning is a tip. Counting toward 2-touch

Experienced

2-touch limit. No scan call = replay the pass

Station B · Coach B · Day 2

Wall pass (give-and-go)

Pass to wall · sprint into space · receive return

A passes to wall B and sprints behind. B returns first-time into A's run. Defender added tracking A's run from Wk2. Works in pairs — everyone involved simultaneously.

New players

Wall holds 1 sec before returning. Sprint to pre-decided space

Experienced

One-touch return. Defender chases from Wk2. Beat defender to receive

Station C · Coach C · Day 2

Press-back 3v3

Lose ball → win it back in 6 seconds

3v3 in a small zone with mini-goals. Losing team must press and win ball back within 6 seconds — coach counts aloud every time. Bonus point for winning ball and scoring directly.

New players

8-second window. No scoring time condition

Experienced

5-second window Wk3. Combination required to score double points

Block 3 · 3-station rotation · transitions and width themes
Station A · Coach A · Day 1 · Weeks 1–4

Win it and play forward

2v1 → defender wins ball → attack in 5 sec

Attacker dribbles, defender wins ball and immediately attacks end line vs 1 recovering opponent. Must reach line within 5 seconds. Teaches instant transition mindset after winning possession.

New players

8-second window. Run direction pre-decided (straight)

Experienced

5-second clock. Spontaneous run. Add recovering defender Wk2+

Station B · Coach B · Day 1 · Weeks 1–4

Number activation 3v2

Coach calls number → that player sprints

3 numbered attackers, 2 defenders. Coach calls a number — that player sprints into space and receives the ball. 3v2 attack to goal develops. Rotate roles every 3 reps.

New players

Run pre-decided (always go centrally). Passive defenders Wk1

Experienced

Spontaneous run decision. 2 numbers called Wk2+. Active defenders

Station A · Coach A · Day 2 · Weeks 1–4

Switch of play

Dribble across · pass wide · cross or shoot

Dribbler carries ball across pitch and plays to wide partner on the far side. Wide player drives to byline and crosses or shoots. Attacker makes timed run into the box.

New players

Any foot. Cross to stationary target in the box

Experienced

Alternate feet each rep. Timed attacker run. Defender closes wide player Wk2+

Station B · Coach B · Day 2 · Weeks 1–4

Wide channel 4v2

Must involve wide player before crossing to goal

4v2 in central zone. One locked wide player each side — protected in their channel. Ball must travel through a wide player before crossing to goal. Teaches width as a tool, not an afterthought.

New players

Wide player receives free — no defender in channel Wk1

Experienced

Closing defender enters channel from Wk2. Combination before wide pass Wk3

Block 4 · 3-station rotation · combination play theme
Station A · Coach A · Weeks 1–4

Wall pass to finish

Give-and-go → receive → first-time shot

Full sequence: pass → sprint → receive return → shoot first-time. Defender tracks A's run from Wk1. Progresses to one-touch throughout for experienced players with active pressure.

New players

2 touches to control before shooting. Wall holds 1 sec

Experienced

One-touch throughout. Active defender from rep 1

Station B · Coach B · Weeks 1–4

Overlap run

Pass wide · run outside · receive in space · deliver

A passes to wide B and immediately overlaps on the outside. B plays ball into A's run. A drives to byline and delivers cross or cuts inside to shoot. Timing is everything.

New players

Coach feeds ball into overlapping space first rep. No defender Wk1

Experienced

B plays first-time. Defender tracks A's run. A must choose: cross early or cut inside

Station C · Coach C · Weeks 1–4

3v2 waves

Combination required before any shot is allowed

3 attackers attack 2 defenders to goal. A wall pass or overlap combination must happen before any shot is taken. After each wave, a fresh group of 3 attacks immediately.

New players

Any 2-player combination counts. 4 touches max among 3 players

Experienced

All 3 must touch ball Wk3+. Shot without combination = disallowed

All Blocks 2–4 · Setup reference

3-station rotation layout

Gold → Blue → White rotate every 15 min on coach's whistle

All 3 teams work simultaneously. On the whistle (every 15 min), all teams rotate. Each team visits all 3 stations = 45 min total. Coaches stay at their station — teams come to the coach. No lines, no waiting.

Timing

15 min × 3 stations = 45 min rotation block per session

Key rule

Coach owns their station for the whole block. Same concept, scaled weekly.
