0 – 5
Juggling — Final Personal Bests 5 min
Block 4 · Final Juggling Session

Personal bests as everyone arrives — players and parents. Parents can try too. Record each player's highest number for the block. Celebrate any player who improved their personal best from Week 1 to Week 5. Brief framing: "This is our last session of Block 4. You've come a long way. Let's make it a great one."

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Light Touch Game 10 min

Keep it fun and easy. Can use a tag game, Shark Attack, or a simple 2-team keep-away with parents joining in. No coaching. High energy and laughter. The goal is everyone loose and smiling before the big game.

Setup Note
Get the Parents vs Kids field set up during warm-up. Full-ish field, 2 real goals or large pugs. Parents team can include coaches. Coaches help balance teams if the parent turnout is very high or very low — a coach on the kids' team is usually a fun twist. Have the sock exchange bags ready at the edge of the field.
15 – 80
Parents vs Kids Game 65 min
The Big Game

Parents vs Kids, full game format. No formal kickoff ceremony needed — just get a ball out and go. Run halves if you like (25–30 min each with a water break), or just play continuously. Coaches call basic restarts but don't officiate heavily — this is a game, not a session.

  • Parents must pass to a kid before shooting. If not enough parents, coaches fill in on the parents' team.
  • Kids: no restrictions. Play freely. This is what all the training has been for.
  • Encourage set pieces when they come up — throw-ins and corners are a moment for the kids to show what they learned. Don't narrate it — just let it happen.
  • Celebrate big moments for both sides. Parents scoring is fine and fun. Kids will work harder for it.
  • Coaches on the sideline can quietly point out good reads: "Did you see that back-foot receive?" — to other coaches, not to players mid-game.
Coach Note · End of Game
At around 75 minutes, start wrapping the game to end by 80. Quick gather — don't let it drag on. The sock exchange and close are the emotional peak of the session. Keep the energy high as you transition off the field.
80 – 90
Sock Exchange + Block 4 Close 10 min
Sock Exchange
  • Everyone gathers in a circle — players, parents, coaches all together.
  • Each player gives one sock to the player on their left and receives one from the player on their right.
  • Short moment of acknowledgment: each player can say one thing they're proud of from Block 4, or coaches can name one specific moment they remember from each player this block. Keep it short — one sentence each is enough.
  • Socks can be fun soccer socks, holiday socks, whatever players brought from home.
Block 4 Close · Coaches' Words
"Block 4 is done." You played 9 sessions. You learned how to build out from the back. You learned how to receive with your back foot. You learned how to defend at corners and throw-ins. You learned how to press and when to hold. You got faster with the ball. You played as a team.

"Every single one of you got better this block. We'll see you next season."
