Tiki-Tak Passing — W3 Progression: Wall Pass in Triangles 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual — Block 3 W3

Groups of 3 in a triangle (cones ~6m apart). A plays to B, B plays a one-touch return to A (wall pass), A continues into the space and plays to C. C is the next A. The triangle moves: all three players shift one position after each sequence. Purpose: the pass-and-move habit — no standing after the ball leaves your foot.

  • Nobody stands still after passing. Move immediately — even one step is enough to signal intent.
  • The wall-pass return must be first touch. B does not have time to control and pass.
  • If the group achieves 5 clean sequences in a row (wall pass + continuation + pass to C), they set a personal sequence record
5 – 15
Warm-Up: Transition Gates — React & Sprint 10 min

Returning warm-up format from W1 S2. Players dribble freely in a large grid. Six cone gates scattered across the space. On "Switch!" call: player must leave their ball, sprint to a new gate, and pick up a different ball. No standing, no slow jogs — sprint to the new gate.

  • Dribble freely between calls. Change direction, change pace — keep the head up to track other players
  • On "Switch!": immediate sprint to a new gate — no hesitation, no looking back at your old ball
  • Progress after 4 minutes: two "Switch!" calls in quick succession — players must sprint to gate 1, then immediately sprint to another gate before slowing down
💡
Coaching tip: The double-switch tests transition off the ball — can they switch tasks twice in a row without a reset breath? That's the cognitive demand we're building toward in the Dog Fight today. No intervention needed; just call the switches and watch.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Aerial Deepened · Full-Sprint Reactive · Pressing Review 45 min · 15 min per station

Station A deepens aerial with foot-served balls and height decisions. Station B adds a reactive/coach-calls-the-gate element to sprint dribbling. Station C reviews all three pressing cues established over the block.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Aerial: Foot Serve + Height Decision

Server uses their foot — a gentle chip or lofted pass from 8m. Player must read the flight and decide: high ball = open chest to trap. Low ball = sole trap. Call out "CHEST" or "FOOT" before the ball arrives. No heading at any point. Coach calls: "Read it. Call it. Then trap it." ⊘ No heading.

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Full-Sprint Dribbling: Reactive Gate

Three numbered cone gates set in a fan, ~12m from start. Player starts dribbling. Coach calls a gate number ("TWO!") at any moment during the first 5m. Player must redirect and hit that gate at full sprint. The decision happens in motion. Rotate through all three players, 3 reps each. Cue: "Head up. Listen. Don't stop to decide — redirect."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Pressing Patience: All 3 Cues Review

3v3 pressing format. Coach calls which cue to demonstrate before each rep: "Close only" (one player closes, two hold shape), "Cover and channel" (channel the ball sideways), or "Full press" (coordinated 3-man wave). Players rotate between press and attack roles. Three reps each cue. Cue: "Which shape are we? Say it before we press."

SSG: Dog Fight — Final Appearance · Minimal Coaching 20 min

Fourth and final Dog Fight of Block 3. Format is embedded — no re-introduction, no coaching cues unless play completely stops. Set it up and let it run. Coaches observe and note individual reads.

Setup — Same as Previous
  • Wide & short field. 3 pug goals each end. Whole team together. 2 balls in play.
  • All restarts apply. GKs play outfield. Restart immediately when a ball goes dead.
  • Coaches: stand back. No verbal coaching prompts. Watch only. Note 2–3 individuals to give feedback on after Knockout.
👁
Observation focus: Who reads the transition first? Who is still looking at the ball when it changes hands — vs. who has already started moving? These are the players who have internalized the block's theme. Note their names — recognise them at the end of the session.

Knockout 10 min
Closing Game + Block 3 Notes

Standard knockout. After the game ends, gather the group for 2 minutes max. Name 2–3 players by name for specific reads you observed during Dog Fight. Keep it specific: "I saw you start moving before the ball even changed hands — that's what we've been building." Then send them home. No overview of the block, no summary — that's for next Tuesday (W4 opener).
