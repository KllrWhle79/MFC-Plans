Tiki-Tak Passing — W1 Progression: Shadow Defender 5 min
Thursday Opening Ritual — Block 3

Groups of 2–3, 2m apart, one-touch passes, high intensity. W1 progression: add a third player as a shadow defender who moves behind the receiver, applying passive pressure. Receiver must weight their feet forward and be ready for the ball.

  • Shadow defender: stands behind the receiver, mirrors their movement — no stealing, just presence
  • Receiver must check their shoulder and still receive cleanly
  • Rotate shadow defender every 30 seconds
  • If a player takes two touches: 5 toe-taps and restart
5 – 15
Warm-Up: Transition Gates 10 min

4 gates placed across a 30×20m area. Two teams play keep-away. When the coach shouts "Switch!" — the team in possession must immediately drop the ball and sprint to defend the nearest gate. The other team picks up the ball and attacks. Pure transition reaction.

  • Start without a ball — players move between gates as a team, coach calls "Switch!" and they reverse direction
  • Add a ball after 2 minutes: team in possession keeps it until "Switch!" then drops it and sprints back
  • No coaching on possession — only reward fast, clean transitions with praise
  • Progress: shorten the "Switch!" interval to every 10 seconds — snap decisions only
💡
Coaching tip: The first "Switch!" will be slow. Don't stop to correct it — call "Switch!" again immediately and build the habit through repetition. Name the first player to sprint back correctly: "That's it — he switched his brain instantly." That's the mindset we're building.

Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Defending Shape: Recovery Run

Same "Side-low-fast-slow" framework from Tuesday. Thursday escalation: defender gets beaten intentionally — must sprint to recover position and reset body shape behind attacker. Drill: get beaten, turn, sprint 5m, get side-on again before attacker reaches the cone. Cue: "Turn — run — reset."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Full-Sprint Dribbling + Chase

Same two-gate dribble from Tuesday. Thursday addition: a chase defender starts 2 steps behind after the player passes the second gate. Player must use a long touch on the outside of the foot to stay ahead. Ball must remain playable — not kicked away. Cue: "Touch — accelerate — hold them off."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Aerial: Foot Serve + Decision

Server now uses foot — gentle lofted pass. Player chests to feet, controls, plays one-touch pass back. Add a decision element: if the ball dips low, use a sole trap; if the ball comes in high, use the chest. Player must read the flight and commit to the right tool. No heading. Cue: "Read the flight. Pick your tool. Stick the landing."

SSG: Dog Fight — Whole Team, 2 Balls, 3 Goals Each Side 20 min

First Dog Fight of Block 3. Wide and short field (~35×25m), 3 pug goals on each end line, whole team (19 players), 2 balls in play simultaneously, all restarts apply. This is loud, chaotic, and unpredictable — that is the design. Communication is the skill being taught.

Dog Fight — Rules & Setup
  • Two balls in play at once. When one goes out, restart immediately from any coach — don't wait.
  • 3 pug goals each end. Attack any of the 3, defend all 3. Choose which goal to attack based on what's open.
  • All restarts apply — throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, everything.
  • Transition is instant. Win the ball → attack immediately. Lose it → get back immediately.
  • LOUD coaching required. Call where the pass is going before you play it. Call "Open left!" if a teammate is free. Call "Get back!" the instant your team loses the ball.
📢
Communication is the point: Stop play once in the first 5 minutes and ask "Who's talking?" Then restart and look for improvement. Name every player you hear talking — positive reinforcement only. If a player scores but nobody called the pass: "Nice goal — but did anyone see it coming? Call it!" Cue: "Talk! Who's open? Get back! Call the ball!"
