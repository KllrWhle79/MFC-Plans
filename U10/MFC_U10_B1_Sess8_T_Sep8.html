Warm-Up: 5v2 Rondo Chain 10 min

Upgraded from 4v1 — same principles, more players, more angles. Groups of 7: 5 outside, 2 defenders in the middle. "Checking!" call mandatory before every receive. Coaches observe without coaching — this is a reading exercise for all three coaches.

  • 5 outside players on a 10×10m square (one player per side + one floater), 2 defenders pressing inside

  • Group target: beat 10 consecutive passes without losing possession
  • Swap both defenders when ball is won, or after 10 passes
  • Coaches: observe who creates the best angles, who talks — note it for positional decisions

45 – 60
Whiteboard + Water: GK Role & Box Rules 15 min

Second-to-last whiteboard of the season. GK connects everything — they've been practising the penalty spot in Knockout since Week 1 without knowing the rules behind it. Draw the penalty area, build on what they already know.

GK Zone — Box Rules

Hands only inside the penalty area. Outside the box they're an outfield player. Build-out line rule: opposing players must retreat behind it when GK has the ball — gives GK time to distribute.

6-Second Rule

GK must release the ball within 6 seconds — but the countdown starts once opponents have moved behind the build-out line. Can't pick up a deliberate back-pass kicked by a teammate. If unsure — just roll or throw quickly.

Distribution

Two options: roll to feet (safest, shortest), throw to chest (medium distance). In U10: rolling to a nearby defender is almost always the right call. Build from the back.

Angle Play

GK steps off their line toward the ball to cut down the angle. The further off the line, the less of the goal an attacker can see. Connects directly to Knockout — they've been doing this as GK every session.

Communication — The Commander (5 min)

The GK runs the defence. Three calls only to teach today:

  • "Keeper!" — GK is coming for the ball, defenders get out of the way
  • "Clear!" — GK can't come for it, defender must clear
  • "Man on!" — warning to a teammate receiving the ball that a defender is close
GK Angle Play — Cutting Down the Angle
💡
Coaching tip: Connect angle play back to Knockout — players have been rotating through GK for 7 sessions. "Every time you stepped up as GK in Knockout, you were doing angle play. You already know this." Then ask: "Who wants to be GK at the tournament?" You'll get hands up. That's your pre-tournament momentum.
60 – 80
SSG: 3v3 Full Rules — Match Conditions 20 min

All rules enforced. Players call restarts before coaches. Coaches referee properly but stay quiet between restarts — let the game breathe. This should feel like a real match. Tournament players get used to pressure; non-attending players get the same quality session.

  • 3 zones of 3v3 running simultaneously — one coach per zone
  • All restarts enforced: throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, free kicks, penalties
  • Offside enforced for the first time in SSG — coaches explain each call once only
  • Players call restarts first — coach confirms or corrects in one sentence, then restart immediately
  • GKs rotate every round — connects to whiteboard session they just had
  • GK communication calls mandatory: "Keeper!", "Away!", "Man on!" — coaches remind once then let players self-manage
  • Round length: 5 min per round, 30-sec water between
  • Final round (75–80): mixed teams — coaches pick the mix deliberately to balance experience
