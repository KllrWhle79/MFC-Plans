Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Fast Dribbling W4 · Lateral Burst

New pattern: player starts at a central cone, dribbles 5m to the right, cuts sharply back through center, continues 5m left, cuts back, then sprints through the final gate straight ahead. Focus on clean directional cuts — toe down, weight shifted, ball stays close. Coach calls "GATE" when to sprint. Cue: "Cut clean. Don't loop it."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Corners W4 · Contested Positions

W4 progression: corner is taken, 2 attackers vs 2 defenders. Defenders must beat the attacker to the goal-side position and hold it as the ball comes in. If defender is in front when the ball arrives, the attacker gets a free header. This is about positioning before the ball is in flight — not jumping at the last second. Cue: "Get there early. Hold the position. Don't watch the kicker."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Back Foot W4 · 2v1 Live

W4 progression: receiver faces a live pressing defender as the ball comes in. Passer plays to the receiver who is being closed down. Receiver must scan before the pass arrives, receive on the back foot, turn, and play out to the third player. No time to pause — the defender arrives 1–2 seconds after the ball. Cue: "Scan early. Receive. Commit to the turn."

SSG: Play Through the Defensive Third 20 min

Standard SSG format (2 goals, field-switch rule). W4 constraint: attacking team must play the ball into the defensive third and receive it back out before they can score.

SSG Rules + W4 Constraint
  • 2 goals, standard kickoffs, full-field switch rule each attacking cycle before scoring.
  • W4 Constraint — Play Through: Before scoring, the attacking team must play at least one pass into the defensive third of the opponent and receive it back out. The ball does not need to stay there — just touch that zone.
  • Forces build-out shape: teams must commit players forward rather than holding possession in midfield.
  • Coaches call "Through! Then shoot!" when a team approaches the box to reinforce the rule.
⚡
W4 Constraint — Defensive Third Touch: Ball must enter the opponent's defensive third and come back out before a goal counts. Defenders: press high to prevent the ball entering. Attackers: use build-out shape to earn the zone.
