Blue · Coach David · Station C — Shield Live (1v1 Contested)

W3 progression: attacker now has the ball at their feet, defender must get to the hand-on-back position and stay goal-side while the attacker dribbles toward goal. No tackling — only shadowing and turning them outside. If attacker gets turned to face goal in a shooting lane, reset. This is the hardest progression: patience over pressure. Cue: "Get side-on. Hand on. Turn them out."

Build-Out: Gold (Read Press) · Blue (Winger Decides Outside/Inside) 20 min

Split-field. Gold on left half, Blue on right. W3 build-out adds Gold's third press shape (drop back and compact) and Blue's winger decision — go outside or cut inside based on defender's position.

Gold (Press Side) · Coach Pete

3 Press Shapes:

1. High Press — GK rolls quickly, forwards sprint onto the ball immediately.

2. Hold Shape — GK rolls, forwards hold their line and wait for the ball to move.

3. Drop & Compact — if outnumbered or ball is played quickly through, entire team drops and sets a defensive block rather than pressing high.

GK calls the shape before every roll. Players must react without further instruction.

Blue (Build Side) · Coach David

Full W3 Sequence: GK roll → CB back-foot receive → CB to outside back → winger receives.

New (W3) — Winger's Decision: Winger looks at the nearest defender before receiving. If defender is tight and in front → go outside (run behind). If defender is off and goal-side → cut inside to the CM.

Cue: "Look before you get it. Outside or inside?"
