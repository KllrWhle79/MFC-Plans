Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Defending Shield (Moving)

W2 progression: attacker now dribbles slowly (jogging pace) in any direction. Defender maintains the hand-on-back position and stays goal-side while the attacker moves. Pairs swap every 5 reps. Defender cannot dive for the ball — wait for the attacker to show it. Cue: "Stay connected. Watch the ball. Turn them outside."

Green · Coach David · Station B — Fast Dribbling T-Shape

T-shape agility pattern with ball: player dribbles straight 10m, side-steps left 5m, side-steps right 10m, side-steps left 5m (back to center), then sprints forward through the final gate. No cones knocked over. Quick footwork, ball stays close during side-steps. Cue: "Quick feet on the sides. Explode through the top."

Blue · Coach Brent · Station C — Back Foot W3 · Turn Through Gate

W3 progression: same cycle as W2 (chasing passer) but now a cone gate is placed 3m behind the receiver. After the back-foot receive and turn, player must dribble through the gate before passing to the next player. Gate forces a full committed turn — not just a half-turn. Cue: "Receive. Turn. Through the gate — then pass."

SSG: Dog Fight — Throw-in Goal-Side Rule 20 min

Dog Fight format (wide/short field, 3 pug goals each end, 2 balls). Throw-in goal-side constraint applied for the first time in a game context.

Dog Fight Rules + Throw-in Constraint
  • Wide/short field, 3 pug goals each end. Two balls in play — all standard restarts apply.
  • Must switch the field at least once before scoring (from Week 1 format).
  • Throw-in constraint: When a throw-in is taken, every defender must be on the goal-side shoulder of their opponent (not in front). If a defender is caught in front when the ball is thrown, the attacking team gets a free ball from the spot.
  • Coaches call violations loudly and immediately — this is teaching, not officiating.
  • Coaches encourage: "Goal side! Shoulder!" before every throw.
⚡
W3 Constraint — Throw-in Goal Side: Defenders caught in front of the receiver when the throw is taken concede possession to the attacking team. Say it out loud before every throw-in. Within 2–3 throw-ins the boys will start self-correcting.
