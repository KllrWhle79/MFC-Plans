Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Defending Throw-ins

Too often our boys are standing in front of the thrower — the ball goes over their head and it's gone. Drill: defender starts in the wrong position (directly in front). Coach calls "Goal side!" — defender must reset to the shoulder before the throw. If they're in front when the ball is thrown, it's a point for the throwing team. Cue: "Shoulder, not in front. Goal side always."

Green · Coach Pete → Brent · Station B — Fast Dribbling

W2 progression — slalom through 5 cones + sprint to gate. Players weave through 5 cones at controlled pace, then burst to a gate 10m ahead at full speed. The transition from weave to burst is the coaching moment — many players slow down before the sprint instead of accelerating into it. Cue: "Last cone — go. Don't pause."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Back Foot Receive W2

W2 progression — passer now jogs after the pass to add chase pressure. Receiver must scan, open up, receive on the back foot, and turn before the passer arrives. The chasing passer creates urgency but should not make contact — they stop 1m away. Full cycle continues. Cue: "Scan early. Turn before they get to you."

Build-Out of the Back — W2 Progression 20 min · Split field

Second build-out session. Same split-field setup. Gold and Blue each have their own half. Build from last Thursday's session — expect smoother sequences from Blue, more press-reading from Gold.

Gold Team · Coach Pete

Introduce a third press shape: "Channel press" — one forward closes the CB, the other holds the channel between CB and CM. GK must read and call it before rolling.

  • Three press shapes now: High / Hold / Channel
  • GK calls the shape, CB responds accordingly
  • Curved forward run: attacker approaches from the side to funnel pressure to the outside, not through the middle
  • Cue: "Name it. Move before they set."
Blue Team · Coach David

Add the winger's decision: does pressure come from outside or inside? Winger must check inside to CM if outside is closed.

  • Defenders apply pressure from outside on 50% of reps
  • Winger reads the pressure and either takes it outside (open) or checks inside (CM)
  • CM must already be in position before winger receives
  • Cue: "Winger — check the outside first. Then decide."
