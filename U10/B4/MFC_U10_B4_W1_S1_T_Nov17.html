0 – 5
Juggling — Personal Bests 5 min
Block 4 Opening — All Sessions

All 19 juggling as they arrive. Count personal bests aloud — players keep their own count and try to beat it each session. Brief intro to Block 4: "This block is about knowing where to be before the ball gets to you. Reading shape. Getting into the right spot." Set the expectation early.

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Transition Gates 10 min

Returning from Block 3. Set up 8 cone gates across a 30×25m area. Players dribble freely and score by dribbling through any gate. Coach calls "Switch!" — players must change direction and find a new gate. Progress at 7 min: add a defender who can close gates (stand in one) for 10 seconds.

💡
Coaching tip: Watch for players with their head down. The gates require scanning — this directly connects to the back-foot receiving station. Cue: "Head up — where's the next gate before you get there."
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Defending Shield · Fast Dribbling · Back Foot Receive 45 min · 15 min per station

All three station threads launch today. Each coach demos their drill in 30 seconds max then goes straight into reps. Whistle every 15 minutes.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Defending the Shield

Intro drill: attacker shields a stationary ball on the edge of a 2m circle. Defender circles slowly and finds the right angle — hand on the attacker's back (legal shoulder contact), eyes on the ball not the player. Rule: defender must be between the attacker and goal at all times. Cue: "Hand on. Watch the ball. Stay goal-side."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Fast Dribbling

Three numbered gates set in a fan 12m apart. Player starts at the base, Brent calls a gate number ("TWO!") at the moment the player takes their first touch. Player must redirect at speed toward that gate and sprint through. Five reps each, one note per player. Cue: "Head up. Listen. React — don't hesitate."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Receiving on the Back Foot

W1 — stationary passer. Passer stands 10m away and plays a firm ground pass. Receiver scans before the ball arrives, opens body to receive on the back foot (weight on front foot, back foot receives), turns and plays to a third player who has moved to receive. Everyone follows their pass and cycles. Cue: "Scan. Open up. Back foot — turn."

💡
Key coaching note — Back Foot (David): The most common failure is the receiver facing their own goal when the ball arrives. Remind them: weight is on the front foot, back foot is the receiving foot, so the body is already half-turned before the ball gets there. If they're receiving with their body square, the drill isn't working yet — stop and reset body position first.
60 – 80
SSG: 3v3 + GK + Target — 2 Goals · Field Switch Required 20 min

New SSG format for Block 4. Smaller field, 2 goals side by side. Teams must switch the field (play to the opposite side) at least once per attacking cycle before shooting. Builds positional awareness and forces combination play.

SSG Rules — Block 4 Format
  • Field size: Smaller than Block 3 — roughly 25×20m. Two small goals, one each end.
  • Field switch rule: Team in possession must play the ball to the other side of the field (switch) at least once before they can score. If they shoot without switching → no goal, GK restarts.
  • Restart: GK rolls out — no punting.
  • W1 constraint: A goal scored directly after a back-foot receive = 2 points. Coaches watch and call it.
  • Rotate target player every 4 minutes.
⚡
W1 Constraint — Back-Foot Bonus: Any goal scored with the shooter having received on the back foot on the play counts double. Coach on each field watches for it and calls "Back foot — 2 points!" when it happens. Coaches call it generously early to teach the reward.
80 – 90
Knockout 10 min
Closing Game

Standard knockout. All 19 in the area, one ball each. Last player in wins. No walking — anyone caught walking is out immediately. Coaches encourage the boys to call out their juggling PBs before the first whistle.
