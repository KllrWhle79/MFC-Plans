5 – 15
Warm-Up: 50/50 Ball Contests 10 min

Continuation of the physical work from Tuesday. Today: 50/50 ball situations — two players go for the same loose ball simultaneously. Gets players comfortable competing for the ball in a controlled, energetic setting. Every player encounters 50/50s in every match.

  • Pairs stand back-to-back, 2m apart. Ball placed halfway between them.
  • Coach calls "Go!" — both sprint for the ball. First to control it wins the rep.
  • No shoulder barge from behind — must arrive side-by-side to contest
  • Winner: shield the ball and hold possession for 3 seconds. Loser: tries to win it back using defending body shape from Tuesday.
  • Progression: pairs face each other 5m apart — coach rolls ball into the space between them simultaneously. Both sprint, arrive together, compete.
  • Final 2 min: triangle passing — 3 players, pass and follow, head up, shake out the legs before the technical block
50/50 Contest · Back-to-Back Start
💡
Coaching tip: 50/50s are where the physical work from Tuesday pays off. A player who goes into a 50/50 upright with straight legs will always lose to one who's low and balanced. When you see a player win because of technique — low hips, body in — stop and name it: "That's not stronger. That's lower." The concept from Tuesday's lean drills is live in a match situation right now.

White · Coach David · Station C — Standing Tackle

Same 1v1 channel. First 4 min: hold shape and step in (same as Tuesday). Last 6 min: introduce the standing tackle — wait for the heavy touch, plant standing foot beside ball, sweep through with the inside of the tackling foot. Both players stay on their feet. New: tackle only on a clear heavy touch. No lunging, no sliding. Exp: steer attacker to one side first, then execute the tackle.

Station C — Standing Tackle (Coach David demo before first group)
  • Same cues as last session: hold shape, show outside, wait for the opportunity
  • The tackle: wait for a heavy touch or head going down — plant the standing foot beside the ball — sweep through with the inside of the tackling foot — full follow-through — both players stay on their feet
  • Cue: "Foot through the ball, not at the player."
  • Watch for: outstretched-leg lunging — stop and reset. The tackle must be timed, planted, and swept — not jabbed.

45 – 60
Whiteboard + Water: Free Kicks & Penalties 15 min

15 minutes. Connect free kicks to Station C (which they just did). Three concepts only. Then connect the penalty directly to Knockout — players already know the spot and the format cold.

Direct Free Kick

Can shoot straight at goal from where the foul happened. Wall must be 10 yards away. GK sets the wall.

Indirect Free Kick

Must touch another player before entering the goal. Given for technical infringements — dangerous play, GK picking up a back-pass. Less common in U10 — mention briefly.

Penalty Kick

Foul by a defender inside their own penalty area. Taken from the penalty spot. GK must stay on the line until the ball is struck.

Connecting to What They Already Know (5 min)
  • Free kick delivery: same technique as the free kick simulation they just did at Station C
  • Penalty: same spot as Knockout — the ritual they've done in every session. "Knockout is basically penalty kicks."
  • Wall rule: 10 yards = roughly 10 big steps away. Ask players to count 10 steps — that's where the wall starts.
  • GK sets the wall by standing at one post and pointing where each defender goes. Practice this on the pitch.
Direct Free Kick — Wall Setup
💡
Coaching tip: The penalty connection to Knockout is the single most powerful thing you can say today. "You've been taking penalties for 6 sessions. You already know how to do this. A penalty in a match is the same thing." Watch confidence immediately spike. It will show in the Knockout at the end of this session.
60 – 80
SSG: 3v3 with Full Restarts + Set Piece Bonus 20 min

Same 3v3 format as Week 3 Thursday — but now players know corner kicks (Tuesday) and free kicks (today's whiteboard). All restarts enforced. Bonus points for goals from set pieces. Coaches referee and let players call restarts first before confirming.

  • 3 groups of 6 play 3v3 simultaneously, one coach per zone
  • All restarts enforced: throw-ins, corners, goal kicks, free kicks, penalties for box fouls
  • Players call the restart first — coach confirms or corrects in one sentence
  • Bonus points: goal from a corner = 2pts · goal from a free kick = 2pts · goal from open play = 1pt
  • Rounds of 5 minutes — rotate opponents after each round
  • Final round (75–80 min): mixed teams — Gold, Blue, White players all mixed
3v3 Zones · Set Piece Scoring
💡
Coaching tip: The set piece bonus points will trigger players to deliberately engineer corners and free kicks. That's exactly right — they're learning to use restarts as a weapon. When a player spots a corner coming and immediately runs to the corner arc to take it, celebrate it: "He recognised it before the whistle. That's game IQ."
