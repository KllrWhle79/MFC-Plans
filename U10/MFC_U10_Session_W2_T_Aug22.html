Opening Ritual — same every session, all season

All 18 players juggling individually as they arrive. Count personal bests. Thighs and feet both count for new players. No instruction — just juggle and count. Freeze Dribble · 25×25m
💡
Coaching tip: On "Go!" after each freeze, deliberately pair players who haven't spoken to each other yet. "Find someone you haven't passed to today." This builds squad cohesion faster than any team-building activity.
20 – 40
Whiteboard + Water Break: Throw-Ins 20 min

20 minutes — cover the three rules, demo on the sideline, then every player attempts one. Move to shaded area; players sit with water.

Throw-In Rules — Three Only (5 min)

Ball over the touchline → throw-in for the team that didn't touch it last.

  • Both hands on the ball — no one-handed throws
  • Ball must come from behind and over the head
  • Both feet on or behind the touchline when releasing

On-Pitch Demo (15 min)

Walk to the touchline. Coach demonstrates once correctly, then once with a deliberate foul throw — wait for players to call it. Then 3 players demonstrate: one correct, one foot over the line, one one-handed. Group referees each one. Finish with every player attempting a throw-in to a partner 5m away. Then run 2–3 live throw-in situations in a small-sided context: ball goes out, right team takes it, receiver calls "checking!" before receiving.

💡
Coaching tip: The foul throw demo only works if you commit to it — one foot over, obviously wrong, then wait. A player who shouts "Foul throw!" before you've finished is exactly who you want. Name that instinct: "That's the awareness we need in a match." The throw-in rules transfer to the technical block that just ran — players who were "checking!" before receiving at Station C were already practising for this moment.
40 – 60
Technical Block: Throw-In Drills — Delivery, Receive on the Back Foot, Check for Pressure 20 min

Second technical block of the season — players know the rotation format from Session 2. Today's three stations all focus on throw-ins: delivery technique, receiving on the back foot, and checking for pressure before receiving. Coaches demonstrate each new drill before the group begins. Rotate on the whistle every 6–7 minutes.

Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Throw-In Delivery

Player stands on a line (acts as touchline), throws to a partner 5m away. Focus: both hands, ball behind and over the head, both feet on or behind the line. Partner calls "Good!" or "Foul throw!" after each rep. Switch roles every 4 throws.

Blue · Coach Brent · Station B — Receive on the Back Foot

Coach or partner delivers a throw-in. Receiver steps back onto their back foot before the ball arrives — weight back, ready to turn either way. Receive the ball, take one touch to set, then pass back. Cue: "Step back, get behind the ball, then receive." No pressure — focus is purely on the receiving shape.

White · Coach David · Station C — Check for Pressure

Same as Station B but with a passive shadow defender standing 2m behind the receiver. Before receiving, player must glance over their shoulder ("checking!") to see where the pressure is. If defender is on the left, receive and turn right — and vice versa. Coach David calls "checking!" as a cue before each throw.

Throw-In Drills — Three Station Focus Areas
💡
Coaching tip: Station C introduces "checking!" for the first time — the habit of looking over your shoulder before receiving. Keep it simple: one glance, then receive and turn away from the shadow defender. Don't over-coach it. A player who does it once without being reminded is ready for the next level. Name that moment loudly when you see it.
60 – 70
Knockout 10 min
Closing Ritual — same every session through Dec 12

All 18 line up. Penalty-style — one shot each. Miss = out, score = survive. GK rotates every round. Eliminated players become the crowd and cheer loudly. New players may shoot from a closer mark; never draw attention to it. Run all the way to a winner.

💡
Coaching tip: End on a high. After the winner is crowned, connect back to the throw-ins they learned today: "Next time you take a throw-in in a match, remember — both hands, over the head, feet on the line. You know this now." One sentence. Then send them off.
