All 19 juggling as they arrive. Count personal bests aloud. Brief preview of tonight: "We're going to split into your teams and work on building out of the back. This is game prep — we're going to get really good at this."

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Pressure Rondo 4v2 10 min

Set up two 10×10m rondos running simultaneously — David oversees both. 4 outside players vs 2 defenders in the middle. Outside players keep possession, defenders press to win it. Progress at 7 min: shrink to 8×8m. Defender who wins it swaps with the passer who lost it.

💡
Coaching tip: This warms up the pressing concepts used in build-out — defenders in the rondo are playing the same role as the pressing team. Remind players: "The defenders in the rondo — that's what we're trying to beat tonight when we build out."
15 – 55
2 Stations: Back Foot Receive · Fast Dribbling 40 min · 20 min per station

Two stations, one per coach. All 19 split into two groups (~9–10 each). Groups rotate at the whistle. No third station tonight.

Coach David · Station A — Receiving on the Back Foot

W1 progression — stationary passer at 10m. Same cycle as Tuesday: scan, open up, back foot receive, turn, play to next. With 20 minutes and larger groups, run two simultaneous cycles side by side. Push for smooth, continuous rhythm by minute 10. Cue: "Scan. Open. Back foot — turn."

Coach Brent · Station B — Fast Dribbling

Three numbered gates, fan pattern. Brent calls the gate number at first touch. 20 minutes with a larger group — keep lines tight, minimum 6 reps per player. Progress at 10 min: Brent calls the number later (at 8m instead of first touch) so players have to adjust mid-stride. Cue: "Head up. Listen. React."

55 – 80
Build-Out of the Back — Gold vs Blue Split 25 min · Split field

First build-out session of Block 4. Field splits down the middle — Gold on one half (Brent), Blue on the other (David). Each team runs their own build-out sequence with their coach. No competition between teams tonight — reps only.

Build-Out Structure (Both Teams)
  • GK starts with the ball at their feet — no punting.
  • GK rolls firmly to the near CB. CB receives on the back foot and gets it wide.
  • Winger receives, looks up — if outside pressure is coming, checks inside to CM.
  • CM positions to offer the third option at all times.
  • Pressing team (3 players) applies light pressure — patience, don't just chase.
  • Reset and repeat. Full sequence 8–10 times before swapping roles.
Gold Team · Coach Brent

Focus on recognizing the press scheme before the GK rolls.

  • Pressing team shows one of two shapes before each rep: "High press" (3 players push high) or "Hold" (drop and screen).
  • GK must call the press shape aloud before rolling.
  • CB adjusts body position based on called press.
  • Cue: "Name the press — now move."
Blue Team · Coach David

Reps-first. Comfort and speed — get the sequence right before adding decision-making.

  • GK roll → CB back foot → outside to winger → winger reads pressure.
  • No calls, no decisions tonight — just the sequence, fast.
  • Defenders press lightly at 50% — enough to make it real, not enough to disrupt early reps.
  • Cue: "Roll → Back foot → Outside."
