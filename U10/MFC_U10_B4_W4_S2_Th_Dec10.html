Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Agility Circuit #2

3-station circuit, repeat twice through:
1. Agility ladder (ball at feet, quick steps through every rung, 10m).
2. T-shape lateral dribble (5m right, back, 5m left, back — keep ball close on the touches).
3. Pressure gate finish — sprint 10m with ball, live defender steps in at the final gate. Defender can only side-step (not full pressure). Player must cut to get through.
Time each rep. Challenge players to beat their own first circuit time.

Green · Coach David · Station B — Fast Dribbling W4 · Lateral Burst

Same lateral burst pattern from Dec 8 (Tue) — right cut, back, left cut, back, sprint through gate. Second round: coach sets up a gate on the right AND one on the left at random, only reveals which one to sprint through after the lateral sequence. Forces real-time decision. Cue: "Quick feet. Wait for the call. Then explode."

Blue · Coach Brent · Station C — Corners Review · Pressure Scenario

Return to corners: corner is taken, 2 attackers vs 2 defenders. Defenders race to goal-side before ball arrives. Add a goalkeeper (a coach or nominated player) calling which side to defend — near post or far post. Defenders adjust their position based on the GK call. 10 reps. Cue: "Listen to the GK. Near or far — get there before the ball."

Build-Out W4: Gold Full Autonomy · Blue Full Sequence + Winger Decision 20 min

W4 is the capstone build-out session. Gold calls and executes all three press shapes without coach prompting. Blue runs the full sequence to the winger's decision. No coach scripting — coaches only observe and name what they see.

Gold (Press Side) · Coach Pete — Observer Only

GK calls the press shape (High, Hold, or Drop) and executes. Forwards react without any coach hint. If a player hesitates, it's a good learning moment to pause and ask "what did GK call?" — not to repeat it. Goal: press can run without coach involvement for 5 consecutive sequences.

Blue (Build Side) · Coach David — Observer Only

Full W4 sequence: GK roll → CB back-foot receive → CB to outside back → winger reads defender and decides outside or inside → CM adjusts position based on winger's decision. New: CM must move before the winger receives (anticipation, not reaction).
