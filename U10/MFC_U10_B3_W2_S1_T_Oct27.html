0 – 5
Juggling — Challenge Progression 5 min
Tuesday Opening Ritual

All 19 juggling as they arrive. Introduce a circuit: inside of one foot → outside of same foot → sole touch → switch. Timed challenge — how many touches before a drop? Personal bests count. Check in with players who were struggling last session.

5 – 15
Warm-Up: Pressure Rondo 10 min

4v2 in a 10×10m box. Defenders cannot dive in — must stay side-on and force the ball to the edge of the box. Rotate every 90 seconds. Primes both defensive stations and builds pressing body shape before the technical work.

  • 4 outside players keep the ball; 2 defenders in the middle
  • Defenders must stay side-on, lean away from the ball carrier, and channel — no lunging
  • Swap the two defenders when they win the ball or after 90 seconds
  • Count passes out loud — group celebrates every sequence of 8+
  • Progress at 7 min: 4v3 — now the outside players really have to move to find space
💡
Coaching tip: Watch for defenders who dive in immediately. Freeze the rep: "Stop — look where you're standing. Are you side-on? Can you see the ball AND the pass lane?" Then restart. One correction per player, then let them work it out.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Pressing Patience · Full-Sprint Dribbling · Aerial Control 45 min · 15 min per station

New station introduced: Pressing Patience (Pete). Brent runs Full-Sprint Dribbling for the third session — add a finish at the end. David carries aerial control into game-realistic scenarios.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Pressing Patience

Two-man press near a corner flag. Presser 1 closes the ball carrier steadily — no lunge. Presser 2 covers the most likely pass lane (usually inside). Together they channel the attacker toward the sideline. Goal: make the attacker go sideways, not forward. Cue: "Close — cover — channel. Don't dive, don't chase."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Full-Sprint Dribbling + Finish

Same two-gate drill with chase defender. New addition: after the player beats the chase, they continue to a small goal and finish. Full sequence: long touch → outrun defender → cut to goal → shoot. The finish should be simple — the point is the dribble. Cue: "Earn the yard. Use it."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Aerial: Game Distance

Server plays a longer lofted pass — simulates a GK roll that travels. Player receives chest-first on the run, controls to feet, lays off to a second player nearby. Pair work: server varies height and pace — player must adjust body position as they move. No heading. Cue: "Win the first ball. Give the second."

Station A · Two-Man Press Shape
Station B · Dribble → Chase → Finish
Station C · Aerial on the Run + Lay-off
💡
Station A — Pressing Patience (Pete): The hardest part for boys this age is doing nothing — closing at pace, then stopping. The goal of the press is to force direction, not to win the ball immediately. If a boy dives in: "You had them — you took yourself out of it." If the pair channels well even without winning the ball: "That's perfect — you made them predictable."
60 – 80
SSG: Dog Fight — Pressing Shape Focus 20 min

Second Dog Fight of Block 3. Same setup: wide/short field, 3 pug goals each end, whole team, 2 balls, all restarts apply. Coaching focus this week shifts from communication to pressing shape — are strikers pressing as a unit, or flying in and leaving their teammates exposed?

Coaching Focus — Pressing Shape
  • Watch the first press: does the closest player close under control, or sprint wildly past the ball?
  • Who covers? When one player closes, someone else should immediately cover the most dangerous pass lane
  • Stop play once for a clear breakdown — freeze it and ask "Who closes? Who covers? Hold!" Then restart
  • Communication from W1 Thu still applies — keep naming players who call loudly
💡
Cue: "Who closes? Who covers? Hold!" Use this as a three-word call you shout from the sideline any time you see good press shape developing. Don't wait for a breakdown. Name the shape while it's happening: "Close — cover — yes! Hold!"
80 – 90
Knockout 10 min
Closing Game

Standard knockout. All 19, one ball each. Sprint to the ball, sprint back into space. No walking — first player caught walking is out immediately. Last two in shake hands.
