5 – 15
Warm-Up: 50/50 Ball Contests 10 min

Continuation of the physical work from Tuesday. Today: 50/50 ball situations — two players go for the same loose ball simultaneously. Gets players comfortable competing for the ball in a controlled, energetic setting. Every player encounters 50/50s in every match.

  • Pairs stand back-to-back, 2m apart. Ball placed halfway between them.
  • Coach calls "Go!" — both sprint for the ball. First to control it wins the rep.
  • No shoulder barge from behind — must arrive side-by-side to contest
  • Winner: shield the ball and hold possession for 3 seconds. Loser: tries to win it back using defending body shape from Tuesday.
  • Progression: pairs face each other 5m apart — coach rolls ball into the space between them simultaneously. Both sprint, arrive together, compete.
  • Final 2 min: triangle passing — 3 players, pass and follow, head up, shake out the legs before the technical block
50/50 Contest · Back-to-Back Start
💡
Coaching tip: 50/50s are where the physical work from Tuesday pays off. A player who goes into a 50/50 upright with straight legs will always lose to one who's low and balanced. When you see a player win because of technique — low hips, body in — stop and name it: "That's not stronger. That's lower." The concept from Tuesday's lean drills is live in a match situation right now.

Station C — Standing Tackle (Coach David demo before first group)

  • The tackle: wait for a heavy touch or head going down — plant the standing foot beside the ball — sweep through with the inside of the tackling foot — full follow-through — both players stay on their feet
  • Cue: "Foot through the ball, not at the player."
  • Watch for: outstretched-leg lunging — stop and reset. The tackle must be timed, planted, and swept — not jabbed.

45 – 60
Whiteboard + Water: Free Kicks & Penalties 15 min

15 minutes. Connect free kicks to Station C (which they just did). Three concepts only. Then connect the penalty directly to Knockout — players already know the spot and the format cold.

Direct Free Kick

Can shoot straight at goal from where the foul happened. Wall must be 10 yards away. GK sets the wall.

Indirect Free Kick

Must touch another player before entering the goal. Given for technical infringements — dangerous play, GK picking up a back-pass. Less common in U10 — mention briefly.

Penalty Kick

Foul by a defender inside their own penalty area. Taken from the penalty spot. GK must stay on the line until the ball is struck.

Connecting to What They Already Know (5 min)
  • Free kick delivery: same technique as the free kick simulation they just did at Station C
  • Penalty: same spot as Knockout — the ritual they've done in every session. "Knockout is basically penalty kicks."
  • Wall rule: 10 yards = roughly 10 big steps away. Ask players to count 10 steps — that's where the wall starts.
  • GK sets the wall by standing at one post and pointing where each defender goes. Practice this on the pitch.
Direct Free Kick — Wall Setup
💡

A penalty in a match is the same thing." Watch confidence immediately spike. It will show in the Knockout at the end of this session.
60 – 80
Positional Play: Building from the Back 20 min · Two groups of 9

First structured positional drill of the season. Split all 18 into two groups of 9 — each group runs their own half-pitch. Teach a repeatable sequence for how the team starts every attack from the goalkeeper. Keep the coaching language simple and consistent: same words, every rep.

The Sequence — Four Steps, Teach in Order
  • Step 1 — GK rolls to a defender: GK receives ball (or starts with it), rolls to one of the two wing-back defenders. No punting. Slow, deliberate. Cue: "Roll to feet."
  • Step 2 — Wing-back takes their space: defender controls the ball, takes one or two touches to settle, and looks up the touchline. Cue: "Take your space, look up."
  • Step 3 — Read the pressure, pick the pass:
    • →Option A (first choice): touchline is open — drive the ball forward along the touchline toward the winger
    • →Option B (second choice): touchline is covered — play inside to the CM (The Glue)
    • →Option C (safety valve): both options are blocked — pull it back to the GK, who switches the ball across to the other wing-back. Start again.
  • Cue for the wing-back: "Touchline, inside, or back." Say it every rep until they say it themselves.
Building from the Back — Decision Tree
Drill Format — Two Groups of 9 · 4 Active, Pressure Players Walk
  • Setup: Half-pitch for each group. GK in goal. Two wing-back defenders positioned centrally. CM in the middle third. Two wingers wide. One or two passive pressure players who walk-shadow the wing-back and winger to create the decision — they do not tackle.
  • Rep start: Coach plays ball to GK (or GK starts with it). GK rolls to one of the two defenders. Rep begins.
  • Run the sequence: wing-back settles, reads the pressure, picks Option A, B, or C. Whichever they choose, coach confirms ("Good — you took the touchline") or redirects ("Touchline was open — try again").
  • First 8 min (coached reps): run the sequence at walking pace with coach directing every rep. Every player gets at least 2 turns as the wing-back.
  • Next 8 min (live — light pressure): pressure players can now jog and apply passive pressure. Players make real decisions. Coaches observe without instructing during each rep — correct only between reps.
  • Final 4 min: free play on the half-pitch — no structure, see if they apply it naturally. Don't coach during this period.
New Players — sequence first
  • Walk-pace reps — get the sequence right before adding pace
  • Use the cue: "Touchline, inside, or back" before every rep
  • One decision at a time — don't rush to Option C
  • GK roll must be on the ground — no punts
Experienced — read and react
  • Pressure player can be active — make the wing-back earn the decision
  • Wing-back must call the option out loud before executing
  • CM must check their shoulder ("checking!") before receiving Option B
  • Option C switch must be played cleanly to the far wing-back's feet
💡
Coaching tip: Teach the sequence in full before the first rep — stand on the pitch and walk through all three options physically. "I'm the wing-back. The pressure comes. Touchline open? I drive. Touchline closed? I go inside. Both blocked? I pull it back and we switch." Then run reps. The cue "Touchline, inside, or back" should come from you every single time in the first 8 minutes. By the end of the live phase, you want the wing-back saying it themselves before they receive the ball.
