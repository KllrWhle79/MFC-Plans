5 – 15
Warm-Up: Sharks & Minnows — Pair Press 10 min

Same Sharks & Minnows as Week 1 — but sharks now press in pairs from the start. No solo pressing allowed. Builds the coordinated press habit before stations.

  • 4 sharks (up from 3 last week), 15 minnows with balls. Sharks must press as a pair — one pressures, one cuts the escape lane.
  • Minnow loses ball → becomes a shark. Last 3 minnows win.
  • Progression (min 10): minnows must complete 3 passes before dribbling — forces scanning under pressure
💡
Coaching tip: Watch for pairs who split too wide and lose communication. Stop once briefly: "Where's your partner? Sharks hunt together." Then restart. The concept transfers directly to Station A today.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: 1v1 Defending · Escape Pressure · 4v2 Rondo 45 min · 15 min per station

Three stations. Week 2 scaling step: defenders are now fully active at all stations for all players. New players face their first live pressure — coaches stay close and give one cue per rep. Demo once, then play.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays. Groups rotate: Gold → Blue → White → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — 1v1 Defending (Narrower Channel)

Same 1v1 channel as Week 1 — but the channel is 2m narrower on each side. Defender has less space to be out of position. Still stays on feet, shows outside, no diving. New: defender fully active from rep 1 — no shadow-only. Make the attacker work for the gate. Exp: defend, win ball, drive immediately to the target cone at pace.

Blue · Coach Brent · Station B — Escape Pressure (Defender Fully Active)

Same corner setup. Defender passes well, follows, and now actively tries to cut off one gate — no lunging, but genuine intent. Attacker must read the pressure and commit to a gate decisively. New: defender actively closes — attacker must make a decision and go. One gate, no hesitation. Exp: 2-touch limit after first touch, defender cuts off chosen gate, attacker drives hard through the other.

White · Coach David · Station C — 4v2 Rondo (Defenders Active)

Same 4v2 rondo. Defenders now fully active — no passive pressure, no slowing down for new players. "Checking!" call mandatory for all. New: defenders fully active from rep 1 — feel the real pressure, no adjustment period. Count every pass sequence aloud. Exp: 2-touch limit, "checking!" mandatory — coach stops play immediately if skipped.

New Players — first live pressure
  • Station A: defender active but not diving — stay on feet, hold shape
  • Station B: defender closes hard — commit to a gate and go, no second-guessing
  • Station C: defenders fully active — count your passes out loud to stay focused
  • Coaches: stay close, one cue per rep, no stops unless safety
Experienced — win it and go
  • Station A: win the ball, drive immediately to target cone — no pause
  • Station B: 2-touch limit, drive hard through the open gate
  • Station C: 2-touch, "checking!" mandatory — one missed call = redo
  • If it feels comfortable, the defender needs to close faster
💡
Week 2 observation: Watch for new players who scan before receiving — they've internalised it from Week 1. Name it immediately and specifically when you see it: "Did you see that? She checked her shoulder before the ball arrived. That's exactly it." It is the payoff of last week's repetition. Experienced players: if they look comfortable, tighten their constraint without warning — "Two touches only, starting now."
60 – 80
SSG: 5v5 + 5v4 — Positional Start & Press 20 min

19 players — 4 teams (5, 5, 5, 4). Two simultaneous games: 5v5 and 5v4. All on field, no subs. Week 2 addition: teams start in their positional shape from Block 1 (2-3-1). Press condition continues from Week 1.

  • Coach sets starting positions before each game — "You're the left wing-back, you're the CM." Takes 30 seconds, not a lecture.
  • Press condition: team losing ball presses immediately. Coaches call "PRESS!" on every turnover.
  • Rotate opponents at 10 minutes.
  • Final 4 min: free play, drop all conditions.
💡
Coaching tip: Don't enforce positions — just set them at the start and see what happens naturally. The moment a player drifts back into shape after a turnover without being told, freeze briefly: "That. He went back to his spot." One sentence. Then let it go.
