5 – 15
Warm-Up: Free Dribble — Ball Mastery Variations 10 min · 3 variations · ~3 min each

All 19 players in a 20×20m area, one ball each, jogging pace — no walking, no running. Keep the ball close. Coach calls variation switch on the whistle. No stopping between variations — transition immediately and keep moving.

Three Variations — ~3 Minutes Each
  • 🟡 Variation 1: Inside → outside → sole of the same foot, then switch feet and repeat on the other side. Keep the ball moving continuously while jogging. Cue: "Same foot — inside, outside, sole — now switch."
  • 🟡 Variation 2: Add a scissor after the inside–outside–sole sequence, then switch sides. Full sequence: inside → outside → sole → scissor, then mirror on the other foot. Cue: "Sequence, scissor, switch — keep moving."
  • 🟡 Variation 3: Two sole touches before the scissor, then switch sides. Full sequence: inside → outside → sole → sole → scissor, then mirror on the other foot. The double sole tap forces balance before the scissor. Cue: "Two soles, scissor, switch — stay on your toes."
💡
Coaching tip: Don't stop to demonstrate — call the cue and let them figure it out. Watch for players who stop the ball between touches — it should always be rolling. If anyone hesitates on the scissor, call out: "Fake the direction — plant and go." Name any player executing all three touches smoothly.
15 – 60
Technical Stations: Defending Shape · Full-Sprint Dribbling · Aerial Control 45 min · 15 min per station

Three stations running simultaneously. All new content for Block 3 — coaches introduce each station with a short demo (30 seconds max) then start reps immediately. No dribbling station this session since the warm-up covers ball mastery.

⟳ ROTATION
Whistle every 15 minutes. Coach stays at station. Groups rotate: Gold → Green → Blue → Gold.
Gold · Coach Pete · Station A — Defending Body Shape

Introduce "Side-low-fast-slow" mantra. Attacker dribbles at defender through a 4m channel. Defender must stay side-on, lean away, and turn and run with the attacker — no foot out, no dive. Cone gates 10m behind defender: defender's job is to force attacker away from the gate. Cue: "Side on. Turn with them. Don't dive."

Green · Coach Brent · Station B — Full-Sprint Dribbling

One long touch on the outside of the foot at full stride. Cone gates spaced 8m apart. Player dribbles through two gates at tempo, then explodes through the last gate at full sprint. The ball should stay playable — not kicked away. Cue: "Touch — gone — catch it."

Blue · Coach David · Station C — Aerial Ball Control

Tossed/lofted serves from a coach or partner. Player positions under the ball, chest traps to bring it to feet — no heading. Progress: chest → control → pass back. Vary height so players must read the flight. Cue: "Make a shelf. Tuck it in."

💡
Coaching tip — Station B (Brent): The most common mistake is pushing the ball too far ahead so it gets away from them. A "long touch" at full stride means the ball stays within 2–3 strides. Demo once at half pace, once at full pace. Then reps. If a player's touch is too heavy: "Softer — control the distance." If too short: "Longer — let it run a little."
60 – 80
SSG: 3v3 + GK + Target Player — Two Simultaneous Games 20 min

First time running this format in Block 3. Walk through the rules before starting — one demo round on each field if needed. 19 players: 4 teams of ~5 (two teams of 5, two teams of 4–5). Two fields running at the same time.

3v3 + GK + Target Player — Rules
  • Ball starts with GK. GK rolls out to Team 1 (building team) — no big punts on restart.
  • Team 1 builds out and tries to reach the Target Player at the far end. Target must "show" — move to get the ball, not stand still.
  • Team 2 presses. If Team 2 intercepts → immediate attack on goal, Team 1 transitions to defense.
  • Team 2 scores → reset: GK starts again for Team 1.
  • Team 1 reaches target → target feeds Team 1 for attack. Team 2 transitions to defense. If Team 1 scores → Team 2 builds out from GK.
  • Rotate target player every 3–4 minutes so everyone gets the role.
⚡
Key coaching focus — transitions: The moment possession changes is the teaching moment. Win it? Go immediately. Lose it? Everyone gets back. Stop play once in the first 5 minutes to name a clean transition, then let it run. Cue: "Win it? Go. Lose it? Get back."

Closing Game

Standard knockout format. All 19 in the area, one ball each. Last player in wins. Sprint to the ball, sprint back into your space after each save. No walking — anyone caught walking is out.
